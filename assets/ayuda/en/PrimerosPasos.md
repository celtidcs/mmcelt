# 🏁 First Steps in MMCelt

Welcome to **MMCelt**! This application is your visual workspace to organize complex ideas, design software projects, and collaborate side-by-side with artificial intelligence models through interactive mind maps.

MMCelt starts from a mind map — a central idea and branches radiating from it — and adds two more things:
named connections between different branches, to express that one item depends on another or blocks it,
and a status and a priority on each branch. This last part is what enables an artificial intelligence
agent to read the map, work on it, and hand it back to you.

A mind map is a graphical representation of your ideas: you start with a **central node** (the core of your project) and branch outward (child nodes to develop topics, sibling nodes for alternatives, and cross connections for dependencies).

### 🎮 How to navigate the canvas and discover help:
- **Pan:** Hold down the **middle mouse button** (scroll wheel) or the **right mouse button** and drag in any direction to move the view.
- **Zoom:** Rotate the **mouse wheel** forward or backward. Zoom centers on your cursor position.
- **Select a node:** Click **once with the left button** on any node box.
- **Center the view:** Press `Ctrl + F` at any time (or go to `🎨 View and Design` → `🎯 Center the View on the Central Node`).
- **🍪 Help Crumbs for Beginners:** MMCelt includes contextual floating notes that discreetly guide you with tips and shortcuts. If you dismiss them and want to see them again, reactivate them anytime from `❓ Help` → `🍪 Help Crumbs for Beginners`.

### 🧭 Where to go next:
1. Click the central node and press `Space` (or double-click) to rename your project.
2. Press `Tab` to create your first strategic pillars (child nodes) and `Enter` to add alternatives (sibling nodes).
3. Open `🤖 Artificial Intelligence` → `🧭 Project and instructions for AI` to explain your project's goal to the AI in the **Project** tab.
