# 📚 Included Examples

Menu **📁 File → 📚 Examples (Complete Maps)**.

Unlike empty template scaffolds, these **come fully populated**: with comprehensive notes, open questions, discarded paths, and cross links.

They exist to resolve initial onboarding hurdles: not how to create nodes, but **how to effectively annotate** a mind map to steer an AI.

### 🎮 Hangman Game
A compact software project: logic, UI, word dictionary, metrics, and unit tests. Designed to fit easily in memory so you focus on map mechanics rather than domain complexity.

### 🍲 Food Delivery Business
Deliberately **non-technical**: product offering, licenses, unit economics, customer acquisition, and logistics. Demonstrates MMCelt is equally effective for business strategy and software architecture.

### 👀 What to observe when exploring:
- Nodes in **❓ Question / Blocker** represent unresolved questions. In Markdown exports, AI receives them aggregated as pending decision points.
- Nodes in **⛔ Discarded** document rejected options, ensuring models do not re-propose them.
- **Cross links** capture dependencies hierarchy cannot: in the business example, "Can I cook from home?" *blocks* cost modeling, since renting commercial kitchen space alters the entire financial structure.
